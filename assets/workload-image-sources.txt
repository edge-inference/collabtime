Workload image sources

The LIBERO, ALOHA and BEHAVIOR-1K images are 1600x1000 stills made for this
website on 2026-11-13 from CollabTime policy runs. They illustrate the
workloads. They are not images of the Thor scheduling comparison.

LIBERO: CollabTime pi0.5 run of LIBERO-Spatial task 0, initial condition 0,
seed 7; the episode succeeds. The policy ran unmodified on its 256x256
observations while the simulator state was logged after each step. The image
is that logged state drawn again with the scene's own side-view camera (field
of view narrowed to 31 degrees) at 2560x1600 with 8x multisampling, then
reduced. It shows the bowl held just above the plate, six steps before the
episode ends in success.

ALOHA: CollabTime pi0 run of gym-aloha AlohaTransferCube-v0, seed 18; the
episode succeeds. The image is the last step of that episode's logged joint
trajectory, drawn with the ALOHA 2 model from MuJoCo Menagerie because the
gym-aloha scene defines no materials. It is not the scene the policy
observed. Arm joint angles and the cube pose are copied unchanged; gripper
link positions agree with the gym-aloha model within 0.003 mm. The ALOHA 2
fingers are 7.6 mm shorter, so the left finger ends 5.5 mm short of the cube
it touches in gym-aloha. The view is from the far side of the table, so the
robot's right arm is on the left of the image.
https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/370c43965323c0944b433b202aac5058b5f907a9/aloha

BEHAVIOR-1K: CollabTime rollout of the pi0.5 policy on turning_on_radio,
public test instance 0, scene house_double_floor_lower, R1Pro robot, in
OmniGibson with no imposed delay. Third-person viewer camera at 2560x1440,
cropped to 16:10 and reduced; the policy's own cameras are unchanged. The
image is step 1,120, with the robot reaching toward the radio. The rollout was
stopped at step 1,150 for the picture and has no success or failure result.
Scene, object and robot assets are part of the BEHAVIOR-1K data bundle and
are shown as rendered by OmniGibson.
https://behavior.stanford.edu/challenge/archive/2025/tasks/00_turning_on_radio.html
